Hi, I am an Assistant Professor in Electrical Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I received my B.E. and Ph.D. degrees from the Department of Electrical Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, and was previously a Postdoctoral Researcher mentored by Prof. Zhijian Jin. I was also a Guest Research Fellow at the Dyson School of Design Engineering, Imperial College London, where I worked with [Prof. Pierre Pinson](https://pierrepinson.com/) on AI-driven modeling, simulation, and decision-making for complex energy and physical systems.

My research focuses on physics-informed artificial intelligence for complex energy and physical systems, with particular interests in data assimilation, generative modeling, uncertainty quantification, stochastic optimization, and trustworthy decision-making. My recent work explores Physical-AI methods for future energy systems, electricity markets, and AI for fusion.
